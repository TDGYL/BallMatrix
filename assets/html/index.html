Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: The app won’t open or keeps crashing?
A: Please try restarting the app or updating to the latest version. If the issue persists, contact us and include your device model and system version.
Q: How do I delete my account?
A: In the app, go to Settings → Account & Security → Delete Account and follow the instructions. After deletion, your data cannot be recovered.
